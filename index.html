Bonzai is a demoscene group which was formed in 1993 by s7ing on the Commodore Amiga. It started with a union of most members of Proline and Savage. The first members where s7ing, Automan, Ted Burning, X-Poole, Mayday, MDB, Dahaul and Solid.

The group was renamed later on in Bonzai Bros. to avoid confusion with the demogroup Bonzai on the Commodore C-64.

More members which teamed up in these years where Ghandy, Gentleman, Ragman, Dr. Avalanche, Toaster, Mr. Vain and Zeroflag. The most active years was the time from 1993 to late 1995 with real Fun and Friendship.

After some unactive years in 2005 a subgroup called Bonzai Revival was founded by s7ing and Dahaul. Later on als Ragman, MDB and Cocoon where reactivated. Bonzai Bros. was reactivated by s7ing in 2025 and maybe more releases and new members will follow.
