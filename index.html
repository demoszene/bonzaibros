Bonzai is a demoscene group which was formed in 1993 by s7ing on the Commodore Amiga. It started with a union of most members of Proline and Savage. The first members where s7ing, Automan, Ted Burning, X-Poole, Mayday, MDB, Dahaul and Solid.

The group was renamed later on in Bonzai Bros. to avoid confusion with the demogroup Bonzai on the Commodore C-64.

More members which teamed up in these years where Ghandy, Gentleman, Ragman, Dr. Avalanche, Toaster, Mr. Vain and Zeroflag. The most active years was the time from 1993 to late 1995 with real Fun and Friendship.

After some years of unactivity, in 2005 a subgroup called Bonzai Revival was founded by s7ing and Dahaul. Later on also Ragman, MDB and Cocoon teamed up. In 2007 the subgroup were closed as s7ing and Dahaul (nowadays know as -pOWL!) joined later on Scoopex&Deviance and after that reactivated Alcatraz on the PC.

Former member Cocoon (nowadays know as Hardy) released some stuff in the years 2007 to 2008 after he joined TRSI. Also Gentleman teamed up again an organized the group for a while. Since then the group went completely inactive.

Bonzai Bros. was reactivated by s7ing in 2026 to have a place for beeing creative and doing some stuff. Maybe more members or releases will follow. Who knows. Time will tell: To be continued!
